✦ Component Architecture & Design Choices

  • Cold Orbit Lines: Hex rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.22) (Ice Sky Blue) ensures clean architectural contrast without clashing with the dark cosmic navy background.
  • Teal Particle Hex: #00F5D4 (Electric Quantum Cyan) with SVG Gaussian multi-stage glow filter.
  • Saffron Particle Hex: #FF9E00 & #FFB703 (Solar Saffron Gold) symbolizing Bharat's scientific energy.
  • 3 Elliptical Orbit Angles: Rotated at -30°, +30°, and 90° (vertical). Gives the iconic quantum atom symmetry while staying sophisticated.
  • Staggered Planetary Speeds: Orbit 1 at 16s, Orbit 2 at 21s, Orbit 3 at 26s. Particles never align repetitively, creating an organic, living quantum state.
  • Strictly Non-Interactive: Uses pointer-events: none and user-select: none so text selection, buttons, and clicks are 100% unimpeded.
  • Zero Heavy Libraries: 100% pure SVG + CSS. Total file weight is < 5 KB, consuming negligible CPU/GPU, ensuring 60 FPS locked on mobile.
  • Reduced Motion Support: Respects prefers-reduced-motion: reduce seamlessly via CSS.
