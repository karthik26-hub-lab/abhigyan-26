Where Ideas Take the Quantum Leap

ABHIGYAN’26 is the 15th National Level Technical Symposium hosted by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Vadapalani Campus. Centered around the theme “Quantum Bharat”, this year’s symposium explores the evolving landscape of quantum technologies and their role in shaping the technological future of India.

ABHIGYAN brings together young innovators, researchers, and technology enthusiasts on a common platform to exchange ideas, challenge conventional thinking, and showcase their skills through a diverse range of technical and non-technical events. From research and problem-solving to creativity, strategy, and innovation, the symposium celebrates the spirit of engineering while looking ahead to a future driven by emerging technologies.
