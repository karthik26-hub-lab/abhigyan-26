## 🌟 Overview
**Abhigyan '26: Quantum Bharat** is the premier annual technical symposium hosted by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Vadapalani Campus. Designed with an editorial, aerospace-grade aesthetic, the platform showcases cutting-edge technical arenas, non-technical challenges, workshops, and registrations.

- **Date**: 24 September 2026
- **Venue**: Auditorium, SRM IST Vadapalani Campus, 100 Feet Road, Chennai - 600026
- **Theme**: *Quantum Leap to Viksit Bharat*

---

## ⚡ Key Highlights & Architecture
- **Kinetic Specular Intro**: Bespoke sequential flash-slam intro typography in minted 24K architectural gold.
- **Asymmetric Editorial Layout**: Desktop 2-column showcase paired with the floating rotating Quantum Ashoka Chakra, with a responsive centered single-column layout on mobile.
- **11 Curated Arenas**: Technical and non-technical flagship challenges across quantum computing, hardware electronics, robotics, circuit design, and gaming.
- **Interactive Venue & Transit**: Transit timeline, walking steps from Vadapalani Metro, interactive detailed address pill, and one-tap Google Maps directions.
- **Continuous Rankings Ribbon**: Dynamic multi-tier rankings ticker featuring SRM's NAAC A++, NIRF, and QS World rankings.
- **Zero Heavy Framework Bloat**: Pure, high-performance vanilla JavaScript architecture with modern CSS variables, fluid clamping, and responsive glassmorphism.
